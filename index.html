02 / Experience

Research and industrial engineering.

A continuous thread: understanding the dynamics, implementing algorithms and testing their behavior.

UAVIA

Oct. 2022 — Mar. 2026

Autonomous Navigation & Control Engineer

Development of drone navigation functions in C++: constrained control, planning, landing and safety.

Contributions & technical details
  • MPC formulated as a quadratic program, using qpOASES and motion constraints.
  • Global Dijkstra planning coupled with local safety corridors.
  • RTK/IMU-based landing logic, phased descent and contact detection.
  • Geofencing, fallback behaviors, terrain following and mission-specific navigation.
  • Integration, simulation, telemetry analysis, tuning and flight testing.
